Courseroad is a place to learn, and a place to teach. This is the first post on our blog, and a short note on what we
are building and how you can follow along.

## Who it is for

- **Learners** work through courses at their own pace and see where they stand.
- **Creators** build and publish courses without needing a team of developers.
- **Organizations** bring their members together, assign roles, and invite new people with a link.

## What you will find here

We will write about the product, about teaching and learning, and about how we build Courseroad. Posts are short and
written by the people who make the product.

<Callout title="Looking for what changed?">
  Every release is listed on the [changelog](/changelog), newest first. You can also subscribe to it as a feed.
</Callout>

## Follow along

Both the blog and the changelog have an RSS feed: [blog feed](/blog/rss.xml) and [changelog feed](/changelog/rss.xml).
Add either to your feed reader and new entries will arrive as we publish them.

Thanks for being here at the start.
